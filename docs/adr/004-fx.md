# ADR 004 — Foreign exchange

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Context

Employees are paid in local currency. Leadership asks “what does the org cost?” in one number. Live FX APIs add an external failure mode with no assessment value.

## Decision

- Seeded `fx_rates(currency, rate_to_usd, as_of_date)` table.
- Org-wide aggregates (headcount cost, percentiles across countries, trends) multiply local amount by `rate_to_usd`.
- Per-country and per-employee views stay in the salary row’s currency.
- Rates are static for the assessment. Updating a rate is a data change, not a product feature.

## Rejected

- Live FX provider: latency, keys, and drift that do not prove product thinking.
- Storing every salary already converted to USD: loses the amount HR actually pays.

## Consequences

- Missing rate for a currency is a 422, not a silent skip.
- Seed includes rates for every currency used by the 10k employees.
